## Projects

### Childcare Affordability in the US
Analyzed county-level childcare price data to measure affordability across the United States. Built static visualizations and an interactive choropleth map to highlight regions where childcare costs exceed recommended affordability thresholds relative to household income.

[View](./Childcare%20Affordability%20in%20the%20US)

### Predicting Diabetic Patient Readmissions
Developed a machine learning classification model to predict whether diabetic patients will be readmitted to the hospital within 30 days. Logistic regression models were evaluated with different class balancing strategies and probability thresholds to improve recall for high-risk patients.

[View](./Predicting%20Diabetic%20Patient%20Readmissions)

### Philadelphia Mobility, Weather & Unemployment Analysis
Combined datasets on mobility trends, weather conditions, and unemployment in Philadelphia to explore how environmental and economic factors influence movement patterns. The analysis focuses on identifying correlations and patterns across time.

[View](./Philadelphia%20Transit,%20Weather,%20and%20Unemployment%20Analysis/)

### Fair Pricing Predictions in the Used Car Market
Built regression models to estimate fair market prices for used vehicles based on features such as mileage, vehicle age, and specifications. The project demonstrates how predictive modeling can support pricing transparency in online marketplaces.

[View](./Fair%20Pricing%20Predictions%20in%20the%20Used%20Car%20Market/)

### Predicting Sleep Quality from Lifestyle Factors
Developed machine learning models to estimate sleep quality using behavioral and lifestyle variables such as physical activity, stress levels, and daily habits. The project highlights how personal health behaviors influence sleep outcomes.

[View](./Sleep%20Quality%20Prediction%20from%20Lifestyle%20Factors/)

### Airport Complaint Trends and Operational Insights
Analyzed passenger complaint data to identify recurring operational issues in airport services. Visualizations and trend analysis reveal patterns in service disruptions and customer dissatisfaction.

[View](./Aiport%20Complaint%20Trend%20Analysis/)

### Kia & Hyundai Theft Trend Analysis Across U.S. Cities
Investigated the rise in theft incidents involving Kia and Hyundai vehicles across multiple U.S. cities. Time-series analysis and visualizations highlight how theft patterns evolved following widely publicized security vulnerabilities.

[View](./Kia%20and%20Hyundai%20Theft%20Trend%20Analysis/)

### Optimizing Sponsored Content on Twitch: Game and Viewership Trends
Analyzed Twitch streaming data to identify games and time periods with the highest viewer engagement. The project explores how viewership trends can inform strategic placement of sponsored content and promotional campaigns.

[View](./Twitch%20Viewership%20and%20Campaign%20Optimization/)

### Modeling Steam Game Prices for Indie Developers
Built predictive models to estimate appropriate pricing strategies for indie games on the Steam platform. The analysis examines how game characteristics such as genre, reviews, and gameplay features relate to pricing patterns.

[View](./Steam%20Game%20Price%20Prediction%20for%20Developers/)

### Predicting Obesity Levels in Latin American Cities
Developed machine learning classification models to predict obesity levels using demographic, dietary, and lifestyle data collected from cities in Colombia, Peru, and Mexico. The project explores how behavioral patterns contribute to obesity risk and how demographic factors influence predictive performance.

[View](./Obesity%20Level%20Prediction%20in%20Latin%20American%20Cities/)
